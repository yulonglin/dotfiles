Tokens are estimated at characters ÷ 4, not a real tokenizer — treat them as ±15% and comparable to each other, not exact. Projected savings assume drop and merge remove a row's whole Tier-1 line and compress removes half of it; edit and keep save nothing. Usage counts are upper bounds. They sum two invocation shapes (the skill field and /slash commands) and fold in pre-rename names, but resumed and compacted sessions copy earlier history into new transcript files, so a busy skill can be counted more than once. A 0 is trustworthy; a large number means “heavily used”, not an exact tally.

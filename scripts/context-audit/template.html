keepEarns its place as it stands. No work.
compressThe job is right, the wording is long. Same file, fewer tokens.
editNeeds rework to do its job — usually it never fires and something must point at it.
mergeFold its content into one named sibling, then delete this one. Each merge row names its destination after the component name.
moveRight content, wrong home — it stays whole, somewhere better. Saves no tokens, since both homes load the same; it buys findability and a coherent split between dotfiles and the plugins.
dropDelete outright. Nothing depends on it.
reviewI did not reach a confident call — read the row and decide.

Tokens are estimated at characters ÷ 4, not a real tokenizer — treat them as ±15% and comparable to each other, not exact. Projected savings assume drop and merge remove a row's whole Tier-1 line and compress removes half of it; keep, edit and move save nothing — a moved skill still loads, just from a better home. Tier 2 (bodies, tok) and Tier 3 (references, tok) are not in this number — you pay those only when a skill actually fires.
Usage counts are upper bounds. They sum two invocation shapes (the skill field and /slash commands) and fold in pre-rename names, but resumed and compacted sessions copy earlier history into new transcript files, so a busy skill can be counted more than once. A 0 is trustworthy; a large number means “heavily used”, not an exact tally.
